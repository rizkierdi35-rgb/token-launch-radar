What “pre-token” means here: the protocol's DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol — it records no token. That is not proof it has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are kept off this board, as are exchanges and versioned pools.

Implied value is this entry's own claim, from the snapshot as of the date shown. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Farm steps are a starting plan, not financial advice: check the protocol's own pages before you move funds, never pay to claim, and never share a seed phrase.
